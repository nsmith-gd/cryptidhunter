# Wood-knock sounds

Source recordings: "Impact Sounds" by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain).
Files used: impactWood_heavy_000-004.ogg (Bigfoot's knocks) and impactWood_medium_000-004.ogg (your knock stick),
retrieved from the CC0 copy vendored in drwhut/tabletop-club (game/LICENSES.tres lists them as Kenney.nl, CC0).

Processing (ffmpeg): Bigfoot knocks pitched down ~18%, low-passed at 2.6 kHz, with a short forest echo tail, mixed to mono.
Stick knocks low-passed at 4.5 kHz with a light echo, mono. No attribution required (CC0), credited anyway.
