# Playtest questionnaire: tension and dread (levels 1–3)

Stub for tester sessions of L01 (Toronto tunnels), L02 (Oro-Medonte) and L03 (Lake Simcoe). Ask straight
after the session, before any discussion. Record the build commit, the level, the seed and the session
length. The automated side of these checks is `npm run check:t134`.

## About you
1. Have you played survival or horror games before? (never / a few / many)

## Tension (1 = calm, 5 = very tense)
2. How tense did you feel in the first five minutes? 1 2 3 4 5
3. How tense did you feel at the furthest point from the car / exit? 1 2 3 4 5
4. How tense did you feel once you got back to the car / exit? 1 2 3 4 5
5. Was there a moment the tension suddenly rose? What happened just before it?

## The named effect of dread
6. Did something about you or the world change the longer you stayed out? Describe it in your own words.
7. Which of these did you notice? (tick any) — fog closing in · night sounds going quiet · your breathing / a heartbeat · your hands shaking (torch / camera) · the torch flickering · the status label (e.g. "Shaken") · none
8. What do you think it was called in the game? (expected: "dread", or a stage name: Watched / Hunted / Dread)
9. What made it go down again?

## The creature
10. Did you see the creature (or something you think was it)? Where, and how far away?
11. Did it ever attack you? (expected in levels 1–3: never) yes / no / not sure
12. Did you feel you could have got closer, or a better photo, if you had done something differently? What?

## Overall
13. Would you go out again for a better photo, or leave it there? Why?
14. Anything confusing or frustrating?

---
Pass criteria for this round (GDD): tension rises with time out and distance from the vehicle and falls on
return; the stalking creature is seen at least once per session; no creature attacks in levels 1–3; most
testers can name the effect (dread) and at least one way it shows.
