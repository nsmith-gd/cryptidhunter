- New greybox map: the Lake Simcoe shore, for the Igopogo (level 3). A dark lake fills the north. A sandy beach runs along a wavy shoreline with a rocky headland, the Point, jutting out in the middle. Mixed pine and birch woods rise to the south, and a gravel side road comes in from the south-east to a parking pull-off, where you start and where the road exit is.
- Two trail loops: the shore loop along the beach and back by the woods edge, and the woods loop inland from the parking. Wading is slow; past waist depth you can't go further, so everything is observed from dry land.
- Three camp spots, each with a cleared pad, a stone fire ring and a signpost: Cedar Cove (sheltered behind a belt of cedars), Rock Shelf (in the lee of a granite ridge) and the Open Beach (exposed, but right on the water). The tent pitches, a fire lights and you can sleep there.
- Three dry lookouts marked with cairns: the Point (a 6 m bluff with a long view over the whole bay), the West Dune and the East Rocks. Three places in the bay where the Igopogo can surface show as faint rings on the water. Each is visible from at least two lookouts (checked by line of sight past the terrain, trees and rocks).
- To see it: open the game with ?map=simcoe. It isn't on the expedition choice yet; it joins when the level has its creature and unlock.
- Assumptions: the layout is my own reading of the GDD's "shoreline loops, sheltered camp spots and observation points" ($150 trip, $1,200 cap noted for later). Greybox only: flat colours and simple shapes, no art pass.
