Added the tagging state machine from GDD 8.2 as a standalone module: Untracked → Tagged → valid telemetry interval recorded → Recovered. A tag only takes during an opportunity window with a compatible tag, and being tagged isn't being captured: the creature stays free. A receiver fix counts only in range, with enough signal, and at least 10 s after the last fix. Three counted fixes in one unbroken watch make a valid interval. More than 60 s without a fix breaks the watch, and the gap is recorded and visible, even right after an interval. The records are only recovered if you get out with them, and only valid recovered intervals pay. Unit tests: 7, including "three valid telemetry updates make one interval".
In the Pine Barrens (#147): tagging the perched Jersey Devil (slot 4, Q) starts a track. Within the loaned receiver's 250 m range, a fix comes every 15 s, and an interval pops up as "Telemetry interval recorded… Get it home." Too far away, the fixes fail ("Receiver: no signal"). Drive out and the track is Recovered: each interval sells as "Tracking telemetry" evidence (GDD EV13). Get caught and it's lost.
How to see it: `npm test` (tagging tests) and `npm run check:t148` (5/5 PASS): Untracked, then tagged on the perch, then an interval in range, then failed fixes and a gap out of range, then driving out, recovered and sold. Captures are in `.ballast/media/t148/`.
Assumptions: the receiver (F17) is loaned with the tag launcher for this contract and has a 250 m range. Fixes come every 15 s of game time. The telemetry's sale price comes from the existing evidence pricing and the Level 9 cap.
