## Questions
### How long should one flashlight battery last?
Context: A battery now lasts about 2 minutes of light, and you start with one spare plus a few lying around the test map. At low charge the beam dims and flickers as a warning before it dies.
Options: - Short, about 1 minute (constant scrounging, more tension) - Medium, about 2 minutes (current) - Long, about 5 minutes (the battery is a rare worry, not a constant one)
Going with: 2 minutes. It's noticeable in a short test session without being a chore; easy to retune once a real map exists.
