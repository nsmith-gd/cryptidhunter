- Each cryptid now has its own behaviour tree, built from its lore first and then from the rules the player has to learn. A panel (F4) shows which branch it's running, e.g. "Bigfoot › Threatened › Warn off".
- Bigfoot (reclusive forest giant):
  - Hears a noise: creeps toward it in stages, stopping dead to listen.
  - Glimpses you: freezes and stares (your photo moment), then often shadows you, pacing ~20 m away in the treeline and sliding out of your light.
  - Every time it spots you, or you hold your light on it, it gets angrier. The first time it just bolts; the next, it rears up, whoops and throws a rock that lands near you (back off!).
  - Push it after that warning and it charges. Corner it up close and it charges at once: at level 1 the first charge is a bluff that stops short with a roar, at level 3 it's real.
- Mothman (Point Pleasant harbinger):
  - In the dark it settles high on a perch and watches with red eyes.
  - Your flashlight draws it down toward you.
  - Hold your beam on it and it swoops at the light: a near miss at level 2, low enough to take you at level 3, never at level 1.
  - Walk under its perch or photograph it and it bursts away to another perch, bolder each time (closer perches, quicker swoops).
- Chupacabra (ranch "goat-sucker"):
  - Freezes while you face it and creeps in when your back is turned; from level 2 it circles to get behind you.
  - Hisses as it coils to pounce, your cue to turn around.
  - Blood bait stops it cold: it goes and feeds.
  - Your light up close drives it back at levels 1–2; at level 3 it just freezes and waits.
- Escalation: each hunting ground is a level. Pine Hollow/Bigfoot is level 1 (forgiving, always warns first), the swamp/Mothman level 2, ranch country/Chupacabra level 3 (fast, flanking, few warnings). Each cryptid also escalates within a night: Bigfoot's anger builds, Mothman grows bolder with each sighting. Add `?tier=1..3` to the address to try any cryptid at another level.

## Questions
### Should Bigfoot's first charge always be a bluff, even at higher levels?
Context: When you corner Bigfoot on the first hunt it charges, stops a few metres short, roars in your face and runs: terrifying, but you survive. From level 3 the charge is real and one grab ends the hunt. Pushing it after its rock warning is always a real charge.
Options: - Bluff first at levels 1–2, real at 3 (current: the danger escalates per level) - Always bluff first, at every level (the warning language stays consistent) - Never bluff (Bigfoot is deadly from the first hunt)
Going with: Bluff first at levels 1–2, real at level 3, which follows "escalation of challenge per level".
