On the Lake Simcoe shore (Level 3, the first overnight trip) you now arrive with a tent and a fire kit in your first two slots. Walk to a camp spot such as Cedar Cove, pitch the tent (slot 1, Q), light a fire beside it (slot 2, Q), step inside and press Q to sleep.
Sleep here lasts until dawn: the night speeds up 16× and you wake at first light. Anything significant wakes you first:
- a splash or a creature's call nearby;
- something coming close;
- getting hurt, dangerously cold, starving or parched.
A splash far across the lake doesn't wake you. The screen says "Asleep until dawn" and what will wake you.
A night's sleep never harms you: while you sleep, hunger and thirst slow down (you're at rest), and if you do get badly thirsty you're woken to drink. Elsewhere (Pine Hollow), sleep still ends when you're rested.
For the lake creature (#136, being built separately): when it surfaces it only has to report "a splash" at its spot and anyone sleeping near the shore wakes up, ready to photograph it.
