## Questions
### Should your torch alone calm you down, or only real light (lamps, fire, the van)?
Context: With the torch on, fear slowly drains while nothing is nearby; with it off in the dark woods it climbs steadily. Lamps and the campfire calm you much faster, and the van is the safest place.
Options: - Torch calms you slowly (current: the torch is your comfort blanket, saving battery has a cost)
- Torch only stops fear rising; only lamps/fire/van lower it (darker, pushes you between light sources)
Going with: torch calms slowly, so the battery-versus-fear trade-off is felt without the forest becoming unbearable.

### How much should terror ruin photos?
Context: At full terror the view sways a few degrees and a photo of Bigfoot that scores 99 when calm drops to roughly 20–55, and the toast says "shaky". Below about a third fear, photos aren't affected at all.
Options: - Strong (current: terrified photos lose about two-thirds of their value)
- Mild (terror costs about a quarter; fear is more atmosphere than penalty)
- Severe (terrified photos are nearly worthless; you must calm down first)
Going with: strong, so steadying yourself before the shot matters.
