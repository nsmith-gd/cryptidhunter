Filled the gaps in the shared cryptid AI from GDD 5.2–5.3. Every cryptid's senses now keep a short memory of what it noticed: sounds, sightings and torchlight, each with a rough position, how sure it is and how long it lasts. Faint or distant sounds are only roughly placed.
Each cryptid also moves through awareness stages: unaware → suspicious → investigating → identified → engaged → lost contact → reset. After losing you it checks where it last sensed you, then gives up and heads back to its range. If it gives up a chase, it forgets you.
Behaviour now comes from data. A species lists hidden traits (fear, curiosity, aggression, territoriality, alertness, habituation) and the shared actions it may use: roam, feed, investigate, observe, flee, warn, pursue, return home. A shared brain scores those actions each moment and avoids flip-flopping; anything frightening right next to a fearful creature makes it bolt at once. Cue types it has checked and found nothing at matter less each time, up to a limit.
Bigfoot runs on the same shared cryptid, senses, memory, navigation and clue trail, and keeps its signature warn-whoop-rock-charge tree. Its behaviour is unchanged; the AI regression checks pass.
A second cryptid plugs in as data alone. The Sidehill Gouger (GDD level 02) is one entry: a four-legged greybox body leaning on uneven legs, acute hearing, a habit of walking one way along its slope, small paired tracks and hair, and it never attacks. A test also defines a new territorial creature purely as data, and it warns, pursues, then goes home.
How to see it: open Pine Hollow with `?cryptid=gouger` and press F4 to watch its stage, remembered cue and action scores. `npm run check:t86` (7/7 PASS) and `npm test` (83/83) cover it; captures are in `.ballast/media/t86/`.
Assumptions: Mothman and the Chupacabra stay as their own one-off cryptids for now. Mothman flies, and the shared framework only has ground bodies so far. The Gouger appears only when asked for in the address, not in any normal hunt.
