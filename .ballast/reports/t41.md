- Ripples: slow, low swells, wind "cat's-paws" drifting across in patches, and now and then a ring spreading from something touching the water. They're drawn on the same 32-pixels-per-metre grid as the textures and animate in steps (10 per second), so they stay crisp and don't swim when you move.

## Questions
### Should the swamp water be glassy and still, or visibly moving?
Context: The water is now nearly still, a black mirror showing trees, lamps and stars, with slow swells, drifting wind patches and the odd ring. The moon makes a glittering path across it. It's calm and eerie rather than lively.
Options: - Glassy and still (current: eerie mirror, strong reflections) - Gently moving (more visible ripples everywhere; reflections break up more) - Moving more when something is near (ripples spread from the cryptid or the player's steps)
Going with: Glassy and still; it suits a dead-calm swamp at night and shows off the reflections. Ripples from movement are a natural next step.
