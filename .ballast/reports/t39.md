## Questions
### How thick should the forest undergrowth be?
Context: Off the trails, the forest floor now has ferns, bushes, grass, fallen logs and rocks. Walking off-trail with the flashlight you push past ferns and step around logs, and the trails stay clear. Clues on the ground can sit among the ferns.
Options: - Sparse (easy to spot footprints, emptier woods) - Current (lush near trails and the creek, thinner deep in the woods) - Dense (beautiful and claustrophobic, but tracks get much harder to spot)
Going with: Current, which looks alive without hiding the tracks you need to follow.
