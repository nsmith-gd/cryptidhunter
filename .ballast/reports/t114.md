New DREAD bar under FEAR: the night now gets scarier the longer you stay out and the deeper you go. Time away from the van, distance into the woods, walking in the dark, every fresh Bigfoot sign you find and a share of every scare push it up. Only a lit, safe place (the van, the cabin, your tent, by a lamp) eases it, and only a little.
It moves through four stages, each announced once by what you notice: Uneasy, then Watched ("The crickets have stopped"), Hunted ("A branch cracks behind you…"), then Dread ("Your heart is pounding… your torch flickers").
It has real effects as it rises: the fog closes in so you see much less far, the edges of your view darken, and the night chorus of crickets dies away under a heartbeat that gets louder and faster. Your fear rises up to 1.8× faster in the dark and near the creature (so more shaky photos). At the top stage your torch stutters for a moment now and then. Bigfoot gets bolder: its escalation goes up a level at Hunted and again at Dread, so it angers quicker and charges for real more often.
How to see it: play Pine Hollow and head deep. The status screen (Tab) shows Dread, its cause and what it's doing to you. Sound starts after your first click (it's skipped with ?mute).

### Should dread ever fully go away during a night, or only ease a little?
Context: Right now dread builds all night. Going back to the van or a lit cabin eases it, but never below 60% of the worst you've felt, so the woods never feel friendly again. A long night ends with thick fog, a heartbeat and a bolder Bigfoot.
Options: - Keep it sticky (the night only gets worse; heading home is the relief) - Let the van reset it fully (a safe base to recover in, then head out again) - Let sleeping in your tent reset it partly
Going with: sticky, because you asked for it to get scarier and scarier as you keep moving.
