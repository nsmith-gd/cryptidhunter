- Press H during a hunt to head back to camp. The hunt ends and a money screen shows your best photo (cryptid and score out of 100), what it earned, and your running total, with a "Hunt again" button (Enter/Space also work).
- Pay is $5 per point of your single best photo (a perfect 100 = $500). Money is kept between hunts and survives closing the page; the HUD shows your current total.
- The camera isn't in the game yet, so until it lands a stand-in pays out: your best "sighting" (how close and how centred a cryptid was in your view). When the camera arrives, any photo it reports replaces the sighting.

Options: - Press a key anywhere (current: quick for testing) - Walk back to your truck at the start point to end the hunt (you must survive the trip out) - A timer: the hunt ends at dawn, with whatever you've got
