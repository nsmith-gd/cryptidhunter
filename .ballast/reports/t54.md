- Replaced the synthesised "tok" with real recordings: free public-domain (CC0) wood impact sounds from Kenney's "Impact Sounds" pack. Bigfoot's knocks use the heavy wood hits, pitched down a little with a short echo through the trees so it sounds like a big branch struck on a trunk; your knock stick uses the lighter wood hits. Five takes each, picked at random (never the same take twice in a row) with a slight pitch wobble, so a run of knocks doesn't sound copy-pasted.
- Far-off knocks are quieter and duller and still come from the right side; the caption and the call-and-response behaviour are unchanged (check rerun: Bigfoot 73 m away answered and came to 21 m; without knocking back it stayed 78 m+).
- Hear it: `npm run dev`, click in, press K to knock; or play the three clips in `.ballast/media/t54/` (Bigfoot close, Bigfoot far, your stick).
- Source and licence are recorded next to the sounds in the game files (credits note): Kenney, CC0 1.0, no attribution required. The usual sound sites (Kenney, OpenGameArt, Freesound) are blocked from this build machine, so the files came from a public project that ships the same Kenney pack with its CC0 licence listed.
- Assumptions: these are table/plank wood impacts reworked to sound like tree knocks, not field recordings of trees; if you want true outdoor tree knocks, someone with normal web access can drop CC0 recordings into the knock sound folder with the same names and they'll be used as-is.
