STATUS: DONE
- Bigfoot's knocks and your knock stick now play real recordings: 10 public-domain (CC0) wood hits from Kenney's "Impact Sounds" pack (5 heavy wood impacts, 5 medium). Each was cut to a single dry hit and loudness-matched. Bigfoot uses the heavy hits pitched down with a short echo through the trees; your stick uses the medium hits from the same set, so both sides of the call-and-response sound like one family of knocks.
- A run of knocks picks a different take each time with a slight pitch wobble, so "tok… tok-tok" never repeats identically. Far knocks are still duller and quieter and come from the correct side; all of that is now applied as the sound plays rather than baked into the files.
- If the recordings ever fail to load, an improved synthesised knock (a sharp strike plus a hollow body with uneven wooden overtones) plays instead, so a knock is never silent. The automated tests also use this path.
- Call and response is unchanged (rerun: Bigfoot 79 m away answered and came to 24 m; without knocking back it stayed 55 m+). Hear it: `npm run dev`, click in, press K; or play the clips in `.ballast/media/t54/` (Bigfoot close, Bigfoot far, your stick, and the synth fallback).
- Sources: Kenney (kenney.nl), "Impact Sounds", CC0 1.0. The original files are impactWood_heavy_000–004 and impactWood_medium_000–004. kenney.nl, OpenGameArt and Freesound are blocked from this build machine, so they came from the unmodified copy of the pack in a public GitHub project, whose licence file lists them as Kenney, CC0. The source, author and licence for every file are recorded next to the sounds in the game files.
- Assumption: these are wood impacts (plank and board hits) rather than field recordings of a club on a living trunk. To swap in true tree-knock recordings later, drop CC0 files into the knock sound folder under the same names.
