## Questions
### How strong should the moonbeams be?
Context: Pale shafts of moonlight now fall through gaps in the trees over clearings and trails. They're gentle: you notice them most when facing the moon, and they never light the forest floor much, so the flashlight is still your main light.
Options: - Subtle (current: atmospheric, the night stays dark) - Strong (dramatic god-rays, a brighter and more fairytale forest, less scary) - Only in a few hero spots (the hollow, the cabin, the lookout), so they mark places
Going with: Subtle everywhere, because it keeps the horror mood and the flashlight's importance.
