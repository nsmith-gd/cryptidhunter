- The rule for "is this spot being watched?" now lives in one shared place that any level can use. A spot counts as watched if you can see it yourself (awake, looking that way, nothing in between), or a security camera can. The camera has to be powered, still in place, pointing that way, in range, with a clear line. A camera that's unpowered, removed or blocked by a rock or tree covers nothing.
- The Hidebehind's movement rule is shared too. It creeps through connected cover one spot at a time, always to the next spot, never jumping. It only takes a stretch that nobody is watching right now; if every way forward is watched, it waits.
- The observation gym (G06) now runs on these shared rules, and its full scripted run still passes unchanged: no false coverage, it never crosses a watched corridor, you can sleep with full coverage, the alarm warns before any intrusion, and power loss leaves the cameras blind.
- Captures: top-down coverage maps of the gym (green is watched) for four setups: all cameras on, one camera removed, the power hub flat, and the camera stuck behind the rock with the blind spot showing.
- Checked: unit tests cover camera range, field of view, power, removal and blocking; direct vision (and not while asleep); moves to a neighbouring spot only; and a 200-setup random test that it never takes a watched stretch or skips ahead.
