New greybox Lake Timiskaming (Level 5, the Mugwump): open `?map=l05` (add `&storm` for wind, rain and waves). It has a main basin with a north and an east bay, a pale wadeable rim, and a shore hub with a dock, a rental shed and a heated warming hut. A road leads out from the hub, and a ring of trail posts runs on dry ground all the way round the lake.
The rental boat is moored at the end of the dock. E climbs in, Space starts the outboard, W/S drive, A/D steer, and E again gets you out (onto the dock or shore if it's beside you, otherwise over the side). The HUD shows fuel, speed and how far the boat is heeling over. Fuel burns with throttle; when it runs dry the engine dies and you paddle slowly.
Capsizing: a hard turn at full speed heels the boat right over and warns you to ease off, but in calm water it holds. A bump from below, or storm waves, tips it over and throws you in. Swim to the upturned hull and hold E to right it, or swim for the shore.
In the water: at the surface you swim (slow and tiring, soaking and cold) with no air meter. Hold crouch to dive and an AIR bar appears, with the view going murky. Let go and you surface and get your breath back at once. Run out of air and you start drowning, which drains health only. Exhausted swimmers slip under for a few seconds unless they wear a flotation vest (`&pfd` for now). Going under soaks you through at once and chills you faster than swimming.
