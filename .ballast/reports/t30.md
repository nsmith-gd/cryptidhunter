## Questions
### What should dawn cost the player?
Context: The night lasts 15 minutes. At dawn Bigfoot leaves the area and the clock turns to dawn. You can still walk back to the van and drive home with any photo you took, but you lose the +25% bonus for getting home before dawn.
Options: - Lose the bonus only (current: gentle, matches your "choose to leave" rule) - Lose the photos too unless you're already at the van (harsh: a real race back) - Fear rises fast after dawn, making the walk back tense without taking anything away
Going with: lose the bonus only, so dawn adds pressure without overriding your decision that the hunt ends when the player chooses to leave.
