New FATIGUE bar. It slowly fills while you're awake, faster when you sprint or haul a heavy pack. Past 40 you're tired, past 70 exhausted, and your stamina is capped lower, never below half. The status screen (Tab) names the cause and the fix.
New tent ($110 at the base-camp shop, 2.2 kg). With it in hand, press Q to pitch it on flat, dry, open ground: a few seconds standing still. Inside it you're out of the rain and wind, and you warm up and dry off. Press Q beside it to pack it away.
Rain: add ?rain to the address (or ?rain=0.5 for a drizzle). It soaks you while you're out in it. Your tent, the cabin and the van keep it off, and in the rain your fire kit only catches by your tent.
Sleep: press Q inside your tent. The night speeds up 8× while fatigue drops; a fire nearby and dry clothes make it better. You wake when you're rested, or at once if a creature comes close, there's a knock, whoop or whistle nearby, or you're bleeding, dangerously cold or starving. Sleep never heals wounds, and hunger keeps falling.
Checked in game (npm run check:t104, all pass): cold, wet, tired and bruised in the Pine Hollow rain, a fire wouldn't catch in the open. Then pitch the tent, light a fire by it and sleep. Fatigue 78 → 5, body 35.3 → 37.1 °C, clothes dry; health stayed 80 and energy fell 29 → 25. Bigfoot walking up, and a wood knock, each woke me. Screenshots are in .ballast/media/t104/.

## Questions
### Should sleeping in the tent skip time fast enough to cut into the night's hunting?
Context: When you sleep in your tent the whole forest speeds up 8 times while you rest. A full night's rest from exhausted takes about 70 seconds of night (about 10 real seconds), so it barely costs the hunt. The design doc wants sleep to compete with time spent gathering evidence.
Options: - Keep it quick (sleep is a short recovery stop) - Make sleep cost a real chunk of the night (several minutes off the dawn clock) - Only allow sleep on multi-night trips, once they exist
Going with: quick for now, so the camp loop is easy to try within one night.
