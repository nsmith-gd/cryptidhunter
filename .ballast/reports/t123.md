The Level 1 tunnel creature is now a real, shy animal. It rests in side chambers and dead ends, then creeps out to one of five lookout spots somewhere ahead of you: the grate's streetlight, a dim old work lamp in the east gallery or the pump passage, the lower junction, or the edge of the steam alcove's glow. There it watches you: a hunched shape at the edge of the light, eyes catching your torch.
If you're careful (light off, slow or still, crouched and quiet) it lingers for up to a minute and even edges a little closer, so you can creep in and get a clear photo in the lamp light. If you shine your torch on it, it rears back for about a second (a last chance for a shot), then chitters and bolts. Walking straight at it, making noise or coming within a few metres also sends it running. It never attacks or chases you.
Every scare makes it wait longer before showing itself again. After three it stays hidden for the rest of the attempt, and the level carries on: escaping is still the goal.
The camera now works in Level 1 (hold right mouse, left-click; 12 shots). Your best photo is graded from the real light on the creature, how steady you were and the framing, and it pays when you get out (up to $88 for an exceptional shot, within the trip's $250 limit).
