- Fear now only comes from cryptids that can actually hurt you. Each cryptid is marked threatening or not. Bigfoot (it can turn and charge), the aggressive test prowler, the Mothman and the Chupacabra (both can catch you) frighten you. Shy, harmless ones don't: one can walk right up to you without a jump scare, without fear rising, and without the "crashing away" scare when it bolts.
