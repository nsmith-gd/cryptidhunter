- Added a Mothman stand-in: a dark sphere with two glowing red eyes that circles high (about 14 m up, 20+ m away) over one end of the test map.
- While your flashlight is on, it's drawn toward you: over about 8 seconds it spirals down until it circles just a few metres above your head, staring at you. Switch the light off and it drifts back up and away over about 7 seconds.
- In the automated run it was 28 m away when the light came on, 4 m away after 12 seconds, and back out to 30 m 10 seconds after the light went off.
- To see it: `npm run dev`, click to play, look up for the red eyes, then press F and watch it come to you. Screenshots in `.ballast/media/t7/`.
- Assumptions: it's harmless for now (no attack or penalty when it reaches you); it's in the shared greybox test map, since the swamp/bridge area doesn't exist yet; you don't need to point the light at it, just have it on.
- PR: https://github.com/nsmith-gd/cryptidhunter/pull/4

## Questions
### Should Mothman be drawn in by the flashlight simply being on, or only when it's pointed toward the sky?
Context: Right now any time your light is on, the flyer slowly spirals down to you, wherever you aim. That makes the light a constant trade-off between seeing and being found.
Options: - Light on at all (current: simple, constant pressure) - Only when the beam points upward or at it (more skill-based; you can search the ground safely) - Both, but aiming at it pulls much faster (lets players deliberately lure it for a photo)
Going with: Light on at all, the simplest to read for a greybox test.
