STATUS: DONE
- Set up the game as a browser game (Three.js); it renders at a low resolution and scales up crisply, the starting point for the 3D pixel-art look.
- First-person controls: WASD to walk, mouse to look, Shift to sprint (about twice walking speed), hold Ctrl or C to crouch.
- Greybox test map: flat checkered floor, walls, scattered boxes, a low slab you can only get under by crouching, and a longer low tunnel.
- You can't stand back up while under the slab; you stand up automatically once you crawl out.
- To see it: `npm install && npm run dev`, open the page, click, walk straight ahead to the low slab and hold Ctrl to crawl under. Screenshots in `.ballast/media/t1/`.
- Assumptions: web engine because no engine was installed or chosen; crouch is hold (not toggle); no jumping yet (not asked for).
- PR: https://github.com/nsmith-gd/cryptidhunter/pull/1

## Questions
### Should crouch be held down or tapped on and off?
Context: In the test map you hold Ctrl to stay low and let go to stand. Crawling through long low spaces means holding the key the whole way.
Options: - Hold to crouch (current: quick ducks, tiring for long crawls) - Tap to toggle (easier long crawls, easy to forget you're crouched)
Going with: Hold, the usual default for tense stealthy movement; easy to add a toggle option later.
