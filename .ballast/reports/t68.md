STATUS: DONE
- Two new survival systems, with ENERGY and BODY bars under your fear bar and warnings that name their cause:
  - **Energy (hunger):** drains slowly (a full reserve lasts about 30 minutes of walking), faster when you sprint or are cold. You carry a packed lunch (+40) and two trail snacks (+15); press Z to eat, and the meal soaks in over a few seconds. Below half: "Hungry: stamina recovers slower. Eat something (Z)". It bottoms out at "very hungry", never starvation.
  - **Body temperature and wetness:** wading the creek or swamp soaks you, and wet clothing loses heat much faster. Dry on a cold night you stay comfortable. The campfire, the cabin, the tent and the van dry you out and warm you back up ("warming (campfire)"). Chilly, then "Cold: shivering, stamina recovers slower. Get dry and find a fire". It never goes below a tutorial floor (34.6 °C): it hurts your stamina, never kills you.
- Each system owns only its own numbers. Cold makes you burn energy faster, and hunger and cold together slow stamina recovery (down to a quarter at worst), following the GDD rule.
- How to see it: `npm run dev` with `?map=forest`, stand in the creek, then warm up at the campfire east of the trail; press Z when hungry.
- Verified (`npm run check:t68`): 90 s dry on a cold night stayed comfortable with no warnings. 2 minutes in the creek: soaked 100%, 35.6 °C, with "Wet clothing: losing heat faster" and "Wading" warnings. 20 more minutes in the water: cold and shivering, stamina recovery at 25%, but no death and the hunt went on. 3 minutes at the campfire: dry, 37.2 °C, warnings cleared. Pressing Z at 30 energy brought it to 69 and used the lunch. The determinism, fear, hunt, base camp and movement checks still pass. Captures in `.ballast/media/t68/`.
- Assumptions: night air 6 °C in Pine Hollow and 13 °C in the swamp; you're wearing the everyday jacket from the GDD starter kit; food is a fixed per-hunt kit for now (base camp doesn't track or sell food yet).

## Questions
### How punishing should getting cold and wet be on the first hunt?
Context: Standing in the creek soaks you in about ten seconds. After two minutes you're "chilly", and after much longer you're "cold" and shivering, with stamina refilling at a quarter of the normal speed. You can never die of cold on this first map, and a few minutes by the campfire or in the cabin fixes it.
Options: - Gentle (current: a nuisance that teaches you to stay dry and use fires)
- Harsher (cold arrives faster and also slows your walking)
- Real danger (cold can eventually knock you out and end the hunt)
Going with: gentle, matching the GDD's forgiving tutorial values.
