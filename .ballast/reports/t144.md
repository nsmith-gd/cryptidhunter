- New greybox map: the Ozark Mountains, for the Ozark Howler (level 7). Two steep, wooded ridges face each other across a valley. Pine Ridge stands between the trailhead and the valley; Bald Knob, the high north ridge where the Howler dens, rises beyond it. Hollow Creek winds along the valley floor. The ridge flanks are steeper than 1-in-1 in places, with impassable rock bands along them.
- Two ways home from the valley junction, signposted both ways: the Bald Route goes straight over Pine Ridge's bare crest. It's 87 m, steep and open, with nothing above you on top, so you're skylined. The Gap Route follows the creek east and climbs through a low, wooded saddle: 182 m, gentle, and over three-quarters of it under trees.
- Four camps, each with a cleared pad, fire ring and signpost: Creek Bend in the valley trees, the Bench on a shelf up Bald Knob, Spring Hollow near the Gap, and Bluff Top, exposed on Bald Knob's crest with a wide view south.
- A deer carcass in the valley gives off scent all night. The scent-following stand-in predator from the previous task dens on Bald Knob. The trailhead ends the trip.
- To see it: open the game with ?map=ozark. It isn't on the expedition choice yet.
- Checked in game: the map loads, there are four camps, and both routes are walkable end to end with nothing blocking (the Bald Route is about 1 minute's walk, the Gap Route about 2). The carcass smells and leaving at the trailhead works.
