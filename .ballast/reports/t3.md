## Questions
### Should clue hints give exact compass directions, or vaguer ones the player has to interpret?
Context: When you examine a track, the message says which of eight compass directions the creature went, and a compass strip at the top of the screen shows your facing. Right now the hint is always exactly right, so the trail is easy to follow once you find it.
Options: - Exact compass directions (easy to follow, feels like guided tracking)
- Landmark wording like "toward the creek" or "uphill" (more immersive, needs the forest map's landmarks)
- Exact for fresh clues, vaguer for old ones (rewards finding fresher signs)
- No direction at all: the footprint shapes are the only guide (hardest, most "real")
Going with: exact compass directions for the greybox, since the test map has no landmarks yet.
