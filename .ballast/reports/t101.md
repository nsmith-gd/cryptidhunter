STATUS: DONE
- The field journal (J) now carries a dossier for each species: Lead → Located → Documented → Characterized → Confirmed, with a tick for each milestone reached, your career credibility, and a plain line on what the next milestone needs. Tagged and Captured exist for later expeditions.
- Milestones only come from trips you drive home from. Located: one clearly identifying sign (a huge footprint), or two weaker signs on different trips. Documented: a grade 2+ record of the target (a photo scoring 40+, or bagged physical evidence). Characterized: two different behaviours observed (a knock exchange, a warning whoop or thrown rock, a charge). Confirmed: strong evidence from two trips and two kinds of evidence (score 90+).
- Credibility is earned once per milestone (Located 1, Documented 2, Characterized 2, Confirmed 3, Tagged 2, Captured 4) and is never spent. An ambiguous record, like a blurry photo of Bigfoot, earns nothing, and the end screen says so. A new RESEARCH section on the end-of-hunt screen lists milestones reached and credibility gained.
- Map unlocks no longer use the reputation number. Blackwater Swamp opens after a successful Pine Hollow extraction plus Bigfoot Documented, and only while you can afford the trip. A locked map tells you exactly what it still needs.
- Buyers unlock by expedition and credibility; local enthusiasts are always open. Base camp lists the open buyers and the next one to come. Credibility and the dossier also show on the base camp panel.
- To see it: play Pine Hollow, photograph Bigfoot well (score 40+) or examine a huge footprint, drive home from the van, and the swamp unlocks on the start screen. Captures in this task's folder: the ambiguous-photo end screen, the Documented end screen, the swamp unlocked, and the journal dossier.
- Assumptions: until evidence grades (#99) land, a photo's grade comes from its score (40+ is grade 2). A grade from #99 will be used automatically once records carry one. The swamp trip cost ($40) is a placeholder. Selling still goes to the default buyer; the buyer offers themselves are #99/#100's work.
