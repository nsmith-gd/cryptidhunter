- The Igopogo is in Lake Simcoe as a greybox stand-in: a dark long-necked shape with two humps. It lives in the lake and never comes ashore. Through the night it rises in one of the three surfacing spots in the bay for about half a minute, drifts there with its head up, then sinks and rests for a few minutes. Its timing is seeded, so the same world gives the same night.
- It hates a noisy shore. Running or walking on the beach near where it's due, hammering in tent pegs, or a crackling fire close to the water makes it skip that rise. If the shore gets noisy while it's up, it dives within a second. Either way it stays down noticeably longer afterwards. Crouching or keeping still on a lookout doesn't bother it.
- How to see it: open the game with ?map=simcoe, walk to the Point (the rocky bluff), keep quiet and watch the bay. Raise the camera when the head breaks the water. From the Point at 50 m it's a small shape: you can photograph it, but the shot grades low (too dark, too small) until there's zoom or better light.
- Checked in game: a whole quiet night on the Point gave 4 surfacing windows (trace: 2:03, 5:07, 9:24 and 13:31 into the night), and it never left the water. A photo from the Point registers it. Running on the beach while it was up made it dive in under half a second, and it then stayed down about 6 minutes.
- Assumptions: windows are 22–36 s every 2.5–4.5 minutes of the 15-minute night. Any noise heard at its spot at 0.3 or more (on the game's 0–1 noise scale) counts as noisy, with sound carrying three times further over water. Audio evidence and a real model come later. In the capture zoomed "through binoculars" the view didn't actually zoom (the game resets the field of view), so it shows the naked-eye view.
