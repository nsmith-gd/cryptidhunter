- Finished the Pine Hollow texture set: bark, leaf litter, moss, dirt, granite and grass (plus pine needles and grass-tuft cards), all generated by a seeded script kept in the game code (wrap-around noise and shapes, colours snapped to the master palette from the style guide), so they're reproducible and tile with no seams. They're imported with crisp nearest-neighbour sampling, no mipmap blur, at one consistent density of 32 texels per metre.
- New this round: checked every tile repeated 3×3 as well as 2×2. No seams, but the 3×3 view showed the leaf litter's and dirt's big dark blotches repeating, so I flattened their large-scale contrast. The forest floor now reads as continuous ground instead of a grid.
- Applied across the whole forest: litter floor, dirt trails and clearings, darker creek banks, granite ridge, boulders and stones, bark trunks, needle canopies, moss and grass patches that break up repetition. The bark and rock materials are the same ones the foliage and prop kit (#39) uses.
- Before/after captures from the same spots are in `.ballast/media/t38/` (files starting with before- are the untextured version), plus the 3×3 seam check sheet.
- How to see it: `npm run dev`, open with `?map=forest`, click in and sweep the torch over the trail and trunks.
- Compared against "ex5" (pixel-textured ground) and "ex" (detail revealed by warm light): texture style and density match that family inside the torch beam; outside the beam the night hides most of it by design.
