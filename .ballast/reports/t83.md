STATUS: DONE
- I compared Player Movement & Flashlight with the GDD and built the missing rules:
  - **Walking pace:** wounds and cold now slow you, on top of your pack's weight, combined as the GDD formula says, and nothing can take you below 45% of normal pace.
  - **Stamina:** wounds lower your maximum stamina (never below half). Running out of stamina only stops you sprinting; you still walk at the normal pace.
  - **Rough ground:** wading the creek or swamp is harder work, so stamina recovers more slowly in water.
  - **Stamina bar:** it now appears only while you exert or recover, then fades out, and shows "max 60% (hurt)" when wounds cap it.
- Flashlight rules from the GDD that were already in place and are now checked: your torch gives you away (a creature facing you from 18 m sees you with it on and not with it off), and a dead battery switches the torch off for good but never touches your health or stamina.
- How to see it: `npm run dev` with `?map=forest`; get hurt or soaked and feel your walk slow; sprint until winded and you keep walking.
- Verified (`npm run check:t83`): walk speed 3.2 healthy, 2.6 cold, 2.2 badly hurt, and 1.44 (exactly the 45% floor) with everything at its worst; stamina capped at 56% when badly hurt and refilling only to that; winded after a sprint but still walking at 3.2; stamina recovered 0.63 on dry ground vs 0.53 walking in the creek; stamina bar visible while sprinting, hidden when full; torch-on sight 0.44 vs 0 with it off; dead battery leaves health and stamina unchanged. The movement, flashlight, survival, health, pack, night-vision and determinism checks still pass. Captures in `.ballast/media/t83/`.
- Not changed, on purpose: the GDD's very first level has a flashlight with no battery swaps, but our board's spare batteries stay; the headlamp, climbing and long-term fatigue belong to later GDD levels.
