## Questions
### How long should clues stay visible before fading away?
Context: Footprints last about two and a half minutes, hair five and scratches seven, fading out over the last third of that time. A trail behind a roaming Bigfoot is around 100 m of prints, and it gets much longer when it bolts.
Options: - Short (about 1 min): trails are fresh and urgent, harder to pick up - Current (2.5 to 7 min): a readable trail with clear fresh-versus-old difference - Long (10+ min): the forest fills with tracks and is easier, but less tense
Going with: The current timings; they give a trail you can follow without the map filling up with prints.
