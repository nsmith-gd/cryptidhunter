Bigfoot no longer just runs when it spots you: it stalks you. It holds back at the edge of your light, 15 to 30 metres out at the first level, and circles you through the trees, watching.
It reacts to what you do. Stand still, keep quiet and look away, and it creeps closer, a metre at a time. Turn your light on it, face it and walk at it, or make a racket, and it backs off into the dark. Just stand and watch it, and it circles. Only if you get right on top of it, or sprint at it from close range, does it break and run.
It's still dangerous: light it up or keep pushing and it warns you with a whoop and a thrown rock, then charges, as before. If it loses sight of you, it works from where it last saw or heard you, then gives up. On higher levels (and as dread rises) it stalks closer and for longer.

## Questions
### How close should Bigfoot dare to creep while it stalks you on the first night?
Context: When you stand still in the dark looking away, Bigfoot creeps in through the trees and stops 15 metres out, close enough to make out its shape in your torch if you turn round. On harder nights it comes as close as 8 metres.
Options: - Keep 15 m (unnerving, but you can still get a clear photo and back away) - Let it come to 8–10 m (much scarier, harder to keep it calm) - Keep it at 20 m or more (mostly a shape at the edge of the light)
Going with: 15 m, so the first night teaches you to read it before it gets truly close.
