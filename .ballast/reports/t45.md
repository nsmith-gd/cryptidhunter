## Questions
### Should bounties refresh with new offers, or is it a fixed list you work through?
Context: The board shows three open bounties. When you claim one, the next in a fixed list takes its place, up to five in total; after that it says "All bounties claimed". It's a short ladder of goals that leads you from a first clear Bigfoot shot to a 90+ cover shot.
Options: - Fixed ladder (current: clear goals, finite) - New random offers each night (endless, less authored) - Fixed ladder per cryptid, unlocked as you reach new hunting grounds (ties into progression)
Going with: Fixed ladder for now; it's easy to extend once the GDD says how progression should feel.
