STATUS: DONE
- Enter VR: the start screen shows an "Enter VR" button only when this browser and device can run immersive VR (secure page, WebXR available, and the device says immersive VR is supported). Otherwise nothing changes and desktop play is exactly as before.
- Clicking it starts a VR session. It uses a floor-relative space so your real height counts, or a standing-height local space on devices without one. The game then runs on the headset's own frame timing. If the device refuses, you get a message and stay on desktop.
- In VR your head controls the view directly. The game never pitches, rolls or sways the camera (the fear sway is desktop-only), and desktop-only effects (the pixel pass, the night-vision filter and the water mirror pass) are skipped in the headset for now.
- Safe pause: taking the headset off or opening the system menu, losing tracking, leaving VR, or switching away from the desktop tab pauses the game with a PAUSED screen saying why. Nothing advances while paused: no dawn clock, hunger, battery or cryptid movement.
- Resuming: tracking and headset pauses resume by themselves once you're back. After leaving VR, one click carries on at the desk.
- Verified with a simulated headset (15 automated checks): the button is hidden with no WebXR and when VR is unsupported, shown when supported; entering asks for floor space and falls back; a refused request is handled; each pause cause pauses, and the world clock doesn't move until it's lifted; desktop resumes. The desktop checks (movement, hunt, pixel pipeline, fixed-step loop, night vision, caught, fear) and all unit tests still pass. Captures in `.ballast/media/t74/`.
- Assumptions/limits: this is the entry point only. In VR you still move with the keyboard; controller locomotion, snap turn and world-space menus (GDD 16.2/16.3) are separate tasks. Not yet tried on a real headset (none available here); the start screen and pause text are desktop overlays, so in-headset pause text needs a world-space panel later.
