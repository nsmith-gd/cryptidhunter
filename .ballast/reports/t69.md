- You now carry a 12-litre daypack behind your 4 quick slots. Everything has weight: clothes, torch, camera, slotted gear, packed gear, food and water. Gear in the pack has to fit its 12 litres.
- On the start screen, click an item to cycle it from quick slot to pack to left at camp, and use − / + to set how much water you take. Two bars show pack space and your load as a percentage, with the effect spelled out (for example "walk/run −32% · stamina ×1.51").
- A heavy load slows walking and running and makes running drain stamina faster (walking also refills it more slowly). Speed never drops below 45%, as the design doc requires. The starter kit with 2 L of water costs about 6%; a pack stuffed to 20 kg costs about a third of your speed.
- In the woods, pressing a packed item's key makes you dig for it for about a second, then it swaps into your active slot. You can't dig while sprinting.
- U lightens the load. It pours out a litre of water, or with no water left it leaves your heaviest packed item on the ground. Walk back over it to pick it up again; anything still on the ground when you leave is lost.
- A readout above the quick slots shows pack litres, kg, load % and the current penalty.
- Assumptions: item weights and volumes are prototype numbers. Water is weight only, because thirst isn't in the game yet. Quick slots ride in belt pouches and don't take pack space.
- Checked in game: a heavy pack is slower and drains stamina faster, and unloading helps. Unit tests pass. One older check of the start screen fails because its clicks time out headless; it fails the same way on main.
