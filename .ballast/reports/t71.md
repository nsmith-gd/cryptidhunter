STATUS: DONE
- New status screen: press Tab during a hunt (it pauses the hunt). It shows your stamina, energy, body temperature, wetness, fear and torch battery as bars with numbers and words, plus every condition affecting you. Each condition gives its root cause, what it's doing to you, and what to do about it. A last section says what you can do right now: stamina recovery %, whether you can sprint, whether your aim is steady, and how much light is left.
- Warnings now come before penalties. Every problem first shows a "SOON" warning, then "NOW" once it actually affects you:
  - Getting hungry at 60 energy, before stamina recovery slows at 50.
  - Standing in water → soaking → wet clothing, before you get chilly.
  - Out of breath soon, before sprint locks.
  - Uneasy, before your aim starts to sway.
  - Battery low, before the beam dims.
- Warnings name the root cause and the consequence in words, for example "SOON · Standing in water: your clothes are soaking through: you will lose heat faster. Get out of the water". They never rely on colour or sound alone. The on-screen warning list is now one consolidated list fed by the same rules, instead of one list per system. I also fixed the top-left HUD overlap, where the energy and body rows sat on top of the fear bar.
- To see it: `npm run dev`, `?map=forest`, wade into the creek or sprint, then press Tab. `npm run check:t71` (11/11) drives each system and confirms every warning appears before its penalty. Screenshots are in `.ballast/media/t71/`.
- Assumptions: hydration, injury and fatigue aren't in the game yet (health and injury are being built by another agent), so the screen shows what exists now and will pick up new systems as they land. Tab is the status key.

## Questions
### Should opening the status screen pause the hunt?
Context: Pressing Tab opens a full status page in the middle of the screen, and right now the hunt freezes while it's open, like the design document's rule that menus pause solo play. The journal (J) does not pause, so the two behave differently.
Options: - Pause (current: read safely, matches the design doc) - Don't pause (tense: reading costs you time, like the journal) - Pause the status screen and the journal both
Going with: Pause, as the design document says; the journal can follow later.
