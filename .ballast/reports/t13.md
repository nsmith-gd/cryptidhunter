## Questions
### Should a single touch from a cryptid end the hunt, or should there be a health or fear buffer first?
Context: Right now the first time the Chupacabra's charge lands or you walk into Bigfoot, the screen goes red and the hunt is over. That's harsh but very clear. The assumed design mentions fear/health dropping from encounters before you're finally caught.
Options: - One touch and you're caught (current: simple, very tense) - A few hits allowed, with a health or fear bar that empties (more forgiving, more to tune) - Some cryptids catch on one touch, others only scare you (varies danger by cryptid)
Going with: One touch, since that's what this greybox step asks for. A health/fear buffer fits the next level.
