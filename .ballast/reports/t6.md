## Questions
### Should you ever be able to catch up to Bigfoot on foot?
Context: In the forest Bigfoot bolts once you're about 15 m away and runs faster than your sprint, so chasing it is hopeless. You only ever glimpse it running off, which makes photos hard but sightings feel rare.
Options: - Always faster than you (current: brief glimpses, photos need stealth or ambush) - Slightly slower than your sprint (you can chase it for a close look, less mysterious) - Fast at first, then tires after a few seconds (short chases possible)
Going with: Always faster, which fits "shy and fast to flee"; stealth and clues should be how you get close.
