- New: the first trip, "01 · Cabbagetown Tunnels". You go down an old maintenance stair under a Toronto park; it caves in behind you on your first attempt, and you have to find another way up through about 1.1 km of fictional brick service tunnels. Three landmarks guide you instead of an arrow: a faded heron mural where the tunnel splits three ways, the old pump room with its giant rusted wheel, and an iron grate with streetlight falling through it. Old painted stencils on the walls help too.
- Only hunger and body temperature press on you (no stamina, thirst, sleep, injuries or torch battery). Your packed lunch was flung out of your pack in the collapse; find it near the stair and eat it. A flooded culvert detour soaks and chills you, and holds the optional trace: a tuft of coarse fur on a drain grate. A warm alcove where an old steam main still runs hot dries and warms you. You also carry one change of dry clothes.
- A creature lives down there: a hunched shape with glinting eyes that watches you from the edge of your light, chitters and scuttles into a side chamber. It never comes close and can't hurt you.
- Money: a new career has $250; the trip costs $25. Climb the ladder at the far end and you're out: escaping with nothing clears the tutorial and unlocks the next trip (Sidehill Gouger); bringing the fur out pays $30. The only way to die is staying in the deep freezing water for minutes after the warnings. You then restart at the entry with the same kit and no second fee.
- To play: on the start screen, click "New here? Start with 01 · Cabbagetown Tunnels". The test version is the same page with "?gym" added: a fixed layout, F9 to reset, F7 to save a log of everything that happened. Captures in this task's folder walk through the whole tutorial.
- Timing (checked by a bot walking the real game): the shortest way out takes about 3.5 minutes at the careful tunnel pace; walking every side tunnel once takes about 19. So a first-timer who explores, finds the lunch and warms up should land in the 15–25 minute target with no contact with the creature. This still needs a real person to confirm.
- Assumptions: the starter money and trip price use the career money system already in the game; the fur's $30 is the design document's example price until the evidence-grading work lands. The camera and sketchbook are in your kit but do nothing in this trip yet. The tunnels are an L1 greybox: functional and lit for mood, not final art.
