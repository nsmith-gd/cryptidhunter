Built Level 2, Oro-Medonte Woods. It's listed as Level 2 in the map picker (unlocked after Pine Hollow) and costs $75 per trip. It's a daylight trip: you leave the parked car at 2:30 PM, and dusk falls at 7:40 PM, about 20 real minutes later. The light warms through the afternoon, turns red at dusk, then goes dark; the clock reads "dusk in …" and the HUD says to find your way back with map and compass.
The woods: 360 m square of real rolling hills (up to 17 m, two ridges) with dense pine and dead snags. There's one trail, from the car to the lower campsite; everything else is off-trail. Bass Creek runs into Bass Lake (the shore point: wade in to drink and refill). There's an upper campsite (both campsites have a fire that warms you), the Gouger's rock den under the north ridge, a feeding meadow, and a flagged camera post overlooking it. Deer tracks are the ordinary-animal false trace, and the Sidehill Gouger roams with its own tracks and hair. No combat, and the fear meter stays on. The paper map names the creek and lake.
Teaching: hydration (drink and refill), exertion (slopes slow you and cost stamina), and minor terrain injuries (rushing down a steep slope can turn an ankle: a light injury, with a warning first). E at the car opens the extraction review, and E again drives out (H still works).

## Questions
### How long should a Level 2 trip last before dusk?
Context: you start at the car at 2:30 PM in afternoon light, and the sun sets at 7:40 PM, about 20 real minutes later. After dusk it gets properly dark, and finding the car with map and compass gets much harder; the trip doesn't end on its own.
Options: - 20 minutes as now: enough to find signs and get back if you keep an eye on the clock
- 30–40 minutes: a calmer outing with more time to track
- 15 minutes: tight, with real pressure to turn back early
Going with: 20 minutes, inside the GDD's 25–45 minute target once you count walking back after dusk.
