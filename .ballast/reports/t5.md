STATUS: DONE
- Added the shared cryptid behaviour: a brown placeholder capsule wanders the map at a walk, stopping for a few seconds at each spot before picking a new one.
- Get within 10 m of it and it turns red and bolts at full sprint pace (6.5 m/s, faster than your sprint), steering around boxes and walls. It keeps running until you're 20 m back, then calms down and wanders from wherever it ended up.
- To see it: `npm install && npm run dev`, click in, and walk toward the capsule ahead to your right (the bottom-left HUD shows its state and distance). `npm run check:t5` replays it automatically. Captures are in `.ballast/media/t5/`: first-person shots plus an overhead GIF (red/orange disc = cryptid, ring = 10 m flee range, cyan = you).
- Assumptions: the team is on a browser (Three.js) build, which has no built-in navmesh, so I added a walkable grid baked from the map's obstacles with pathfinding. Each cryptid size gets its own grid, so tall ones can't squeeze under low gaps the player crouches through. At this level it reacts to distance only (no sight, hearing or sound) and only flees, never attacks. Its look and speeds come from a per-cryptid profile that Bigfoot (#6) will reuse.
- PR: https://github.com/nsmith-gd/cryptidhunter/pull/3
