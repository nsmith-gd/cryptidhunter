- New test gym, G07: capture, containment and cargo. It's a small capture yard in the woods, with a stand-in specimen (a hunched Dogman-sized shape) on a fixed routine: it wanders, then comes to feed at the anchor post. That's your clearly signalled opening, and it leaves again after a few seconds.
- It contains everything the GDD lists: a light capture module (rated for it, 2 charges) and a heavy one (rated for something far bigger, so incompatible here) on a rack; a transport crate with a repair kit; a generator powering the crate's restraint; a cargo sled on a route to a truck (the delivery point), with a fallen log that can block it; and a separate foot path out.
- Controls in the gym: 5 commit the light module (Shift+5 the heavy one), 6 secure and crate it, 7 repair the crate, 8 emergency release, 9 stage on the sled or deliver at the truck, 0 generator on/off, L drop or clear the log. The side panel shows the capture state, the integrity and condition gauges, charges, crate repairs, generator, route and payouts.
- To see it: open the game with ?map=g07. "Run script" plays every task and marks the pass criteria.
- Checked by the scripted run:
  - Committing with no opening spends nothing.
  - The wrong module during an opening spends exactly one charge, fails, and the creature turns hostile.
  - The right module spends exactly one charge, and a second press spends nothing.
  - The creature stays one individual in the crate.
  - With the generator off, integrity falls and warns below 40, and a repair restores it.
  - The log blocks staging, with the reason given.
  - Delivery at the truck pays exactly once.
  - Nothing is delivered if it escaped, failed or wasn't loaded.
  - After releasing a specimen you still walk out the foot path with your evidence.
  - A reset replays to the identical result.
- Known: the objective text at the top still says "Photograph Bigfoot" (carried over from the hunt); it means nothing in the gym.
