Added a headless career ledger. Money is integer cents in CAD, a new career opens with $250, and the L01 trip cost ($25) is defined. Every purchase, payout and bonus is one transaction keyed by a unique ID, so posting the same ID twice is a no-op. A charge you can't afford is refused, so cash never goes below zero.
It covers expedition attempts (committed once, settled once; a retried debrief returns the first settlement), deposits held apart at 20% and returned once less capped damage, resale at floor(0.5 × list × condition), repair at ceil(0.35 × list × restored), and emergency fees collected only up to available cash.
Unit tests reproduce the GDD 3.7 ledger (250 → 225 → 375 → 245 → 170 → 620 → 375 → 225 → 925). They also check no double charge, no resale arbitrage, no payout for unextracted records, and 100 seeded random careers.
In game, base camp and the shop read the ledger: buying bait posts one $15 charge, and driving home settles the hunt once under its attempt ID. Settling it again pays nothing, and the balance survives a reload. See it with `npm run check:t97` (6/6 PASS) and in the captures in `.ballast/media/t97/`.
Assumptions: the $25 trip debit is not charged yet; #98 charges it at Commit. Repaired condition resells at the repair rate so a repair can never be resold for a profit. Old saves keep their money if it's above $250, otherwise they start at $250. Bounties pay once ever, keyed by bounty ID.
