## Questions
### How big should your hands be on screen?
Context: Your gloved hands now hold the torch (bottom right) and the camera (bottom left). Because the game renders at a low resolution, they're about a fifth of the screen tall; at that size the fingers and stitching read only roughly.
Options: - Current size (more forest visible, hands are a frame) - Larger and closer (hands and gear show their detail, more "cinematic first-person") - Higher-resolution rendering just for the hands (crisper, but breaks the uniform pixel look)
Going with: current size, keeping the whole screen at one pixel size; bringing them closer is a one-line change.
