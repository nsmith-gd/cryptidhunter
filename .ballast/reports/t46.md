## Questions
### Are the shop prices in the right range?
Context: A good Bigfoot photo earns roughly $100–$250. The first level of each upgrade costs about one good night's pay ($80–$110), and the top levels cost two to three nights' pay. Bait and batteries are cheap enough to restock every night.
Options: - Cheaper (most upgrades in the first few hunts, faster power curve) - Current (one upgrade a night early on, all maxed after roughly 10–15 good hunts) - Pricier (upgrades are big choices; you'll pick one path first)
Going with: Current, a steady sense of progress each night without maxing out too soon.
