STATUS: DONE
- You can now fail a hunt in each way the design document lists, and each has its own ending screen. CAUGHT: the creature's blows take your health to zero. BLED OUT: you leave a heavy wound unbandaged ("You bled out from the wound Bigfoot gave you"). FROZEN: you stay in deep, cold water past the warnings. Hunger alone never kills you; it only weakens you.
- Cold is now a real danger outside the tutorial: deep water chills you far faster than before. First comes "Dangerously cold: soon you'll start losing health" (about a minute and a half in), then "HYPOTHERMIC: you are losing health" as your health drains, until you collapse about two minutes later if you stay in. Get out and warm up, and the loss stops. On a wet night in Pine Hollow the cold only slows you; Blackwater Swamp's deep channels are where it can kill.
- Not making it out now costs what the design document says. You lose everything you carried, anything you'd set out in the woods, your own gear on you (bought boots, for example) and rented gear (the deposit is kept). You keep the gear you left at base camp, your banked money, your evidence archive and research, and the places you've unlocked. The death screen lists exactly what was lost and what was kept. There's no medical bill for a death, and the free starter kit is there again next time.
- Coming home hurt costs a small, visible recovery charge: 5% of the trip for a moderate injury, 10% for a severe one, never more than the cash you have. It shows in the debrief, and you start the next trip at full health.
- To see it: let Bigfoot catch you in Pine Hollow with extra gear packed, or wade into the deep water in Blackwater Swamp and stay there. Captures in this task's folder.
- Assumptions: the starter kit (3 bait, 2 trail cameras, a battery, torch, camera) is a free hand-me-down the camp tops up, so a death really costs the extras you bought. Drowning isn't in yet because there's no swimming; falls aren't either, because there's nowhere to fall from.
