- Field infrastructure now has a physical state, for the level-12 floodplain operation and beyond. Each structure and machine has integrity: it warns below 40, and at 0 it's broken and does nothing until repaired. Structures that carry loads have a weight limit: the staging pad, the cargo bridge, the winch, and a light footbridge. Machines (the generator, the cargo winch) can be started and stopped. Only running, working machines vibrate, which is what the creature homes in on.
- Damage stays local. A blow to the shelter changes nothing else on the site. If the generator is wrecked, only what runs off it goes dark (the winch, the floodlights), and the bridges, pad and footbridge are untouched.
- Routes check what they depend on. The heavy cargo route needs the pad, the winch (powered) and the cargo bridge, each able to bear the load, and gives the reason if not (for example "the cargo winch can't take 2,200 kg"). The pedestrian escape depends only on the footbridge. A site where the foot exit would rely on cargo equipment is rejected outright, so a flooded, broken or overloaded cargo route never closes the way out on foot.
- This is the rules layer, with nothing new to see in game yet; the floodplain map and the creature come with the level's other tasks. Capture: a scenario trace. Load the cargo, the creature smashes the shelter (nothing else touched), then breaks the running generator: the winch and floodlights go dark and the cargo route closes. Repair it, then a flood blocks the cargo track and the cargo bridge is swept away. The foot exit stays open throughout.
- Checked by unit tests: damage stays local; a power loss reaches only what runs through it; the warning and break thresholds; repair reopens a route; weight limits, with reasons; the foot exit survives every cargo failure; a site with a foot exit that depends on cargo equipment is rejected; and vibration comes only from running, working machines.
- Assumptions: greybox numbers (pad 3,000 kg, cargo bridge 2,500 kg, winch 2,000 kg, footbridge 300 kg). Repairs use the existing repair systems later.
