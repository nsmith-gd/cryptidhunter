STATUS: DONE
- New map, Pine Hollow Forest (greybox): a 300 x 300 m night pine forest (about 1,750 blockout pines plus dead snags and ferns) with dirt trails linking the landmarks. South: the trailhead where you start (truck behind you). Middle: the old cabin (walk-in, door on the east, porch, woodpile, a warm lamp glowing in the window), an abandoned campsite with smouldering embers, and a creek winding across the whole map: wading it slows you, the footbridge on the main trail keeps you dry, stepping stones mark a west ford. North: a rock ridge spans the map with a main pass and a far-west notch; past it, a fire lookout tower with a lit cab (the tall landmark) and Bigfoot's hollow (boulder ring, fallen log, nest), which is now where Bigfoot spawns on this map.
- How to see it: `npm run dev`, add `?map=forest` to the address, click in and follow the trail north. The test yard is still the default map so other checks keep working.
- Verified (`npm run check:t9`): with the real walk controller the player walked 252 m from the trailhead to Bigfoot's hollow and 280 m from the west edge to the east edge; the footbridge crossing stays dry, wading elsewhere is slower. Bigfoot, sent from south of the ridge to a point straight north of it, routed 140 m around through the west notch and arrived without clipping anything. All other agents' checks still pass. Captures in `.ballast/media/t9/` (trailhead, cabin, footbridge, ridge pass, lookout tower, hollow, flashlight off, overhead map).
- Fix along the way: the cryptid movement used to give up its route when a step clipped the corner of a blocked spot, which happened constantly between dense trees; it now slides along instead.
- Assumptions: ground is flat; the ridge is a wall of rock blocks (not climbable) rather than real hills, since the controller has no slopes yet. Off-test-map rules currently also bring in the Mothman and stalker placeholders here. Greybox (L0) only: compared against target "ex" (dark interior with warm practical lights), only the lighting mood is aimed at (warm cabin window, campfire embers and lookout lamp against cold blue-black fog); detail, materials and pixel-art polish are far below that bar, as expected at greybox.

### Should the ridge be climbable hills or a solid rock wall with passes?
Context: A rock ridge cuts the forest in two; you can only cross at the main pass on the trail or a notch far to the west. That forces Bigfoot and the player through chokepoints, which is good for ambushes and tracking.
Options: - Solid wall with passes (current: clear chokepoints, easy to read)
- Real sloped hills you can climb anywhere (more natural, needs slope movement first)
Going with: solid wall with passes at greybox.
