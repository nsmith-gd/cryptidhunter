Lake Timiskaming (`?map=l05`) now has a shared power and fuel budget, shown in a new POWER & FUEL panel at the bottom left: each battery with the devices running off it, the fuel in litres, and the generator.
Two batteries share the load and fail separately. The boat battery runs the sonar (5, each ping costs charge) and the boat's lantern (6). The power bank runs the GPS (8, shows your position), the hydrophone (7) and the underwater camera (records by itself while you're under water). When one goes flat, only its own devices go off and the panel says why. Everything else keeps working, including your torch (its own cells) and the paper map, and nothing in your body changes. Press 9 to move a flat battery's devices onto one of two spare packs.
Fuel: the outboard burns petrol from its own tank (8 of 12 L at the start). When it runs dry the engine dies; aboard, 0 pours the 5 L can in. At the rental shed, 0 starts the generator, which burns its own small tank and charges the power bank while you stand there and the boat battery while the boat is tied up at the mooring.
Checked by `npm run check:t140`, all 9 checks passing with the real keys pressed. Captures are in `.ballast/media/t140/`.
Assumptions: charge is shown as percent, and the drain rates are tuned so a careless night runs a battery flat in roughly 10–20 minutes. The sonar shows depth only, with no creature returns, until the creature proxy exists. Devices you moved to a spare stay on it after the main battery recharges. The hydrophone draws power but doesn't record sounds yet. The shop doesn't sell spare packs or fuel yet: every trip starts with the same kit.
