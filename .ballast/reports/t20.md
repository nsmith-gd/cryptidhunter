- Added a field journal. Aim at a clue (footprint, hair or claw marks) within arm's reach of about 3.5 m and "[E] Examine …" appears under the crosshair; press E and it's logged with a short toast. Each clue can only be logged once.
- Press J to open the journal: a paper notebook page listing each clue you've found (newest first) with its type, a short description (e.g. "Huge bare footprint, 45 cm long") and how old the clue is. The age keeps ticking, so you can tell fresh tracks from stale ones.
- After three clues it names the likely cryptid with a confidence rating and a bar per suspect, e.g. "Likely cryptid: Bigfoot, High confidence (96%)". Before that it says how many more clues it needs. The third clue's toast also says "looks like Bigfoot".
- The verdict weighs what each clue suggests: huge prints, brown hair and high claw marks point to Bigfoot, while small prints and low scratches would point to the Chupacabra. Mothman and Chupacabra are suspects even though they don't leave clues yet.
- To see it: `npm run dev`, click in, find Bigfoot's trail with the flashlight, examine three clues, press J. `npm run check:t20` does this automatically (11/11 pass). Captures are in `.ballast/media/t20/`.
- Assumptions: examining is instant (no inspect close-up or photo, which belong to other tasks); the game keeps running while the journal is open; the journal lasts one session and isn't saved yet.

Context: Pressing J lays the notebook over the middle of the screen, but the night carries on behind it: Bigfoot keeps moving and the other cryptids keep hunting. Reading your notes is a small risk.
