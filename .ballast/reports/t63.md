## Questions
### Should we keep building the Pine Hollow Bigfoot night hunt, or switch the starting adventure to the GDD's Toronto tunnel tutorial?
Context: Everything playable today is a night hunt for Bigfoot in Pine Hollow Forest, with a dawn deadline and a van to drive home in. The new GDD starts instead with a short escape from tunnels under Toronto, where the creature retreats rather than hunts, and Bigfoot isn't on its list of thirteen creatures.
Options: - Keep Pine Hollow and Bigfoot as a playable slice while the GDD's order is built around it
- Switch the starter to the Toronto tunnels and treat Pine Hollow as a later expedition (or retire it)
- Rewrite the GDD to start with Pine Hollow and Bigfoot
Going with: keep Pine Hollow as the playable slice for now; the GDD's appendix lists everything that would change if we switch.

### Do we keep the fear meter, which the new GDD rules out?
Context: Right now a FEAR bar rises in the dark and near dangerous creatures; high fear sways your view a little and makes photos blurry. The GDD says there should be no fear or sanity meter at all, with fear coming only from the atmosphere and real danger.
Options: - Keep the fear meter and update the GDD
- Remove the bar but keep the shaky-photos-when-in-danger effect
- Remove it entirely, as the GDD says
Going with: keep it until you decide, since you approved it earlier.
