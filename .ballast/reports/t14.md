- One full hunt now runs start to finish. A briefing screen sets it up (Pine Hollow, 11 PM, find Bigfoot, get a photo, get back to your truck before dawn) and lists every control. Click to start.
- During the hunt, an objective line at the bottom guides you ("search for tracks, press E to examine" → "follow the trail, click to take a photo" → "got the shot, return to your truck, 19 m"), and a clock in the top-right runs from 11:00 PM to dawn in 7 real minutes.
- Taking the photo: left-click with Bigfoot near the crosshair and in plain sight, up to 35 m away. You get a white flash and "SNAP! Photo of Bigfoot (21 m)". It bolts if you get within about 15 m, so you have to shoot from a distance.
- Your truck sits just behind where you start, with a tall pulsing green light you can see from anywhere. Walk into the green ring with the photo and you get a "HUNT COMPLETE" summary (photo distance, clues logged, journal verdict, time). Other endings: "DAWN BROKE" if time runs out, "HUNT ABANDONED" if you press H at the truck without a photo, and "CAUGHT" if a cryptid touches you. Click or Space starts a new hunt.

## Questions
### How long should a night last before dawn ends the hunt?
Context: The night now lasts 7 real minutes (11 PM to about 5:30 AM on the in-game clock). A quick player can find Bigfoot, snap it and get back in 3 to 5 minutes; a cautious one has some slack. When it runs out you get "DAWN BROKE" and lose the photo.
Options: - 5 minutes (tight, rushed, more failures) - 7 minutes (current: some slack for a first-timer) - 10+ minutes (relaxed exploring; dawn rarely matters)
Going with: 7 minutes, so a first-time player can finish without help while dawn still adds pressure.

### Should the hunt end automatically when you reach the truck with a photo, or should you choose when to leave?
Context: As soon as you step into the green ring around your truck holding a Bigfoot photo, the hunt ends and the results appear. That means you can't go back out for a better shot or more clues once you've arrived.
Options: - Auto-end on arrival (current: simple, clear) - Press a key at the truck to leave (lets you bank the photo and keep hunting for a better one or more evidence)
Going with: Auto-end for greybox clarity; a "leave now?" choice fits better once there's money and evidence quality.
