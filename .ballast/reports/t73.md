Aim at a footprint and press X to pour a plaster cast (3 kits per hunt). It sets in about 4 seconds, and walking away ruins it. A photo with a footprint or claw marks in the middle of the frame is also kept as a footprint photo or disturbance photo. A prompt under the crosshair says what you can do ("[X] Bag hair sample (4 pouches)", "[X] Cast it (3 kits) · photograph it for evidence").
- Each item goes observed → collected → extracted → sold. Drive home from the van and base camp sells everything you carried, at the GDD base values: footprint cast $60, hair sample $45, footprint photo $30, disturbance photo $24. Repeats of the same kind from the same creature pay half, then a quarter. Get caught and it's all lost ("Caught — 1 evidence item lost"). The results screen has an EVIDENCE list and an "Evidence sold" line, and the base camp panel lists each item next time.

## Questions
### Should evidence be worth as much as a good photo?
Context: A great Bigfoot photo sells for several hundred dollars. A full set of physical evidence from one trail (cast, hair, footprint photo) brings about $135, and repeats pay less. So the photo is still the big prize, and evidence is what makes a night without a sighting worth something.
Options: - Evidence as a modest top-up (current)
- Evidence worth about as much as a good photo (tracking alone can fund your career)
- Evidence needed to unlock bounties or reputation (it opens doors rather than paying cash)
Going with: a modest top-up, so failed sightings still pay, as the GDD intends.
