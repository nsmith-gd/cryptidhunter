STATUS: DONE
- New second map, Blackwater Swamp (greybox): a 100 x 100 m misty swamp of murky water with a boardwalk network linking the spawn dock, a central junction, the ranger shack (walk-in, door on the east side), an east fishing platform, a mud island, and the old bridge over a deep channel up to a north platform with a derelict water tower. Dead cypress trees, reeds and small mud hummocks fill the water.
- Wading: shallow water drops you to 60% walking speed, the deep channel and pools to 35%, and you can't sprint in water; boardwalks are full speed. The HUD shows WADING / DEEP WATER. You now step up onto low ledges (boardwalks, stairs) automatically.
- The old bridge is the landmark: stairs up from the island, a deck high enough to wade under, tall end towers with glowing amber lamps that read through the fog. Mothman circles over it. Bigfoot stays in the test map; the low stalker placeholder still roams the swamp for now.
- To see it: `npm run dev`, open the page with `?map=swamp` added to the address, click, and walk north along the boardwalk; step off into the water to feel the slowdown. Automated check (`npm run check:t10`) measured 3.2 m/s on boardwalk, 1.9 in shallows, 1.1 in deep water, and walked dock → bridge → north platform, junction → shack and junction → fishing platform without touching water. Captures in `.ballast/media/t10/`.
- Assumptions: map picked by a web address option until a hub/map select exists; one flat water level (deep water is darker and slower rather than lower); greybox colours only, no pixel-art polish (L0). Compared against target "ex" (dark night scene with a warm light source): only the lighting mood (amber bridge lamps in green-black fog) is aimed at it; detail and materials are far below that bar, as expected at greybox.

## Questions
### Should deep water just slow you, or also be dangerous?
Context: In the swamp, stepping off the boardwalk into the shallows makes you trudge at a bit over half speed; the dark deep channel under the bridge slows you to about a third and you can't sprint. Nothing else happens in water.
Options: - Slow only (current: simple, the boardwalk is just the fast route) - Deep water also makes noise that draws cryptids (wading becomes a stealth trade-off) - Deep water also drains stamina or soaks your gear (harsher, pushes you onto the boardwalks)
Going with: Slow only for the greybox; noise would hook in once the player noise system lands.
