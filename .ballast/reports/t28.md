- Trail camera (T, you carry 2): sets a small camo camera on a stake in front of you, facing where you're looking. It watches a cone about 15 m deep, with a blinking red LED. When a cryptid walks into view it fires an IR flash and you get a notification ("Trail camera 1 caught Bigfoot! (score 41)"), wherever you are.
- Photo score: every photo now gets a 0–100 score from distance and how much of the cryptid is visible. Trail-camera shots score half of the same shot by hand (41 vs 82 in the test). A trail-camera photo of Bigfoot counts as the hunt's photo, and the results screen shows your best photo, its score and which camera took it.
- Audio lure (L, you carry 1): sets a small speaker box on the ground that plays a Bigfoot "whoop" every 12 s for 2 minutes. You hear it with direction and falloff; listen to `04-audio-lure-call.wav`. Bigfoot hears it from up to 90 m, walks over to investigate and lingers while it keeps calling. Other cryptids ignore it (the call is species-specific).
- Verified with the player 50–60 m away: a trail camera photographed Bigfoot walking past; the lure pulled Bigfoot 57 m across the map to within 4 m of it; and a lure with a trail camera pointed at it got a Bigfoot photo with nobody there. The sandbox's hostile test cryptid didn't react to the call.
- To try: `npm run dev`, click to play, press L to drop the lure, turn around and press T to point a camera at it, then walk away and wait for the notification. Screenshots in `.ballast/media/t28/`.
- Assumptions: trail cameras send their photos straight to you (like a cellular trail cam), so you don't walk back to collect a memory card. The keys (T, L) and counts are placeholders until the loadout system (#27) decides what you carry. The call is a synthesized placeholder sound.

## Questions
### Should trail-camera photos complete the hunt on their own, or only support a photo you take yourself?
Context: Right now a trail camera catching Bigfoot counts as your photo: the objective flips to "return to the van" even if you never saw it. Its photos score half of a handheld one, so the reward is lower, but a player could set a lure and camera, wait by the van, and leave.
Options: - Counts as the photo (current: gear is a real alternative strategy, lower payout) - Supports only: needs a handheld photo too (keeps the face-to-face moment central) - Counts, but you must walk back to collect the camera (risk and travel added)
Going with: Counts as the photo, with half score, so the gear has a clear payoff; easy to tighten once money and scoring exist.
