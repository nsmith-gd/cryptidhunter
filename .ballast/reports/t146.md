- New greybox map: Point Pleasant, for the Mothman (level 8). It's an old munitions works gone back to woodland. Gravel works roads in a rough grid are the stable paths, with a cleared yard and a signpost pointing down each road at every junction. Rows of grassed-over concrete storage igloos with rusted doors line the roads. A rail spur runs up the west side, and a pylon line strides along the north edge.
- Readable landmarks stand above the trees so you can find your way when your electronics are dead: the Water Tower in the middle, the Stack (a brick smokestack) to the north-west, and the Hopper (a concrete loading silo) to the south-east. At least one is in sight from every junction and from about 90% of the roads. Two bare overlooks, Slag Hill and Lookout Knob, look out over the works.
- The marked road exit is Route 62, leaving through an open gate with a green road sign at the south. You start just inside it, and it ends the trip.
- The Mothman (still the placeholder shape) now uses the map. With your light off it flies a corridor up the rail spur and along the pylon line. Once it's been dark a while it perches on the tall things: the tower, stack, hopper or pylon tops. Its interference reaches the ground below a perch, so your torch dies underneath it while the paper map still works.
- To see it: open the game with ?map=pointpleasant. It isn't on the expedition choice yet.
