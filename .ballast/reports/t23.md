## Questions
### Should knocking back be risky as well as useful?
Context: Right now answering Bigfoot's knock is all upside: it answers and comes to a safe distance, and you can do it every 12 seconds.
Options: - Safe lure (current: reliable sighting tool) - Each knock-back makes it warier, so the third or fourth one makes it leave the area - Knocking too much makes it come at you angrily instead of curiously
Going with: safe lure for now so the call-and-response is easy to learn; wariness is a good L2 tuning step.
