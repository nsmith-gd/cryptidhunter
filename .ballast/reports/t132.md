Dread now changes how you play, not just how it feels. Once you're shaken (the same point the "Shaken" label appears), and more the worse it gets:
- Your breathing is loud enough for something close to hear, even when you stand still.
- Your trembling hands blur your photos, so they grade lower.
- Pouring a footprint cast takes longer, bagging a sample takes a moment of fumbling, and unfolding the map takes a second or so.
- Your stamina refills more slowly.
Every effect has a cap. Dread never hurts you, never stops your controls working, and never makes the screen show anything untrue: the status screen lists exactly these effects while they apply.
Dread now falls quickly somewhere safe: fastest at the van, then in a shelter (cabin or your tent), by a fire, or under a lamp. From badly shaken to calm takes about 15 seconds at the van. A scare that dread itself causes (say, your breathing draws Bigfoot in) only counts from the next moment, so it can't snowball within an instant.
Checked in game (npm run check:t132, all pass), calm vs badly shaken:
- Standing still you went from silent to a breathing noise Bigfoot heard from 7 m.
- The same photo dropped from quality 94 to 85.
- Casting went from 4 s to 6 s, bagging a sample from instant to about 1 s, and unfolding the map from instant to 1.3 s.
- Stamina refilled at 79% of its normal rate.
- Health never moved.
There are also unit tests for each effect. Measurements and screenshots are in .ballast/media/t132/.

This replaces the earlier rule that dread only eased a little and never fell below 60% of its peak, since the design now says it should fall quickly.
