- Blackwater Swamp is a playable second map. Boardwalks run over black water to a ranger shack, a fishing platform, a mud island with a huge old cypress, and the old bridge over the deep channel, with Mothman's platform beyond. Wading is slow and the deep channel slower. Green-grey fog banks, a low moon, a warm lamp at the shack and fireflies light it.
- New this task: the swamp now has things to find. Mothman leaves claw gouges and snapped limbs high on cypress trunks beside the boardwalks, where it lands to perch, and smears of glittering grey "wing dust" on the boards of its favourite perches (the bridge, the north platform, the fishing platform, the island). Examine them for the journal, photograph them, and scrape the scales into a sample pouch. A couple of owl feathers are real false leads.
- A whole hunt plays: start at the dock, follow the sign, photograph the Mothman when it perches, drive out, and HQ grades what you brought home. Checked in game: claw marks examined, wing dust photographed and bagged, Mothman photographed on the bridge, hunt completed and verified.
- To get there: it's on the expedition choice as Blackwater Swamp (target: Mothman). It unlocks after a successful Pine Hollow extraction with Bigfoot Documented.
- Known gap: in the claw-mark screenshot the pale gouges are hard to pick out against bright torchlit bark, though examining them works. The Mothman is still the old placeholder shape (a dark sphere with red eyes).
- Assumptions: the GDD's level 2 is a woodland, and the swamp and Mothman sit later in it, but this board keeps Blackwater Swamp as the second map, so I built it as specified here. The extraction vehicle still parks just behind the starting dock, as before.
