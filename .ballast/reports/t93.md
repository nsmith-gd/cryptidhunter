- The Chupacabra is now a real creature instead of a brown box: a gaunt, hairless, dog-sized predator with mottled grey-green hide, a paler ribbed belly, a hunched back crested with blood-tipped quills, swept-back ears, a long fanged muzzle, glowing red eyes, a thin whip tail and hooked claws. It stands about 0.6 m at the back, so it skulks low through the reeds.
- It moves: when still it breathes, sniffs the air, turns its head and flicks its tail. It creeps in a crouched trot while stalking, breaks into a bounding run when it charges, and coils low with its jaws open just before it leaps, so the pounce is telegraphed.
- To see it: start a hunt in Blackwater Swamp. It roams the boardwalks and stalks you when your back is turned.
- Compared against the director's Bigfoot reference: I aimed for the same bar of a menacing face with glowing eyes and worn, mottled hide. It's close in mood, but the face is a smaller canvas, so it reads by its eyes, fangs and quills rather than facial detail. Captures: studio turntable, face close-up, idle, walk, run and crouch animations, and in-game torchlight shots.
- Assumptions: the GDD has no Chupacabra entry, so the design follows the lore the game already uses for it (stalks from behind, hisses, shies from light). Its existing stalk-and-pounce behaviour is unchanged; only its body and animation are new.
