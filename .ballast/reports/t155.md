- New finale rehearsal space, G09: a short stand-in for the last expedition, built from systems the game already has. You start at a trailhead in the south (also the last way out) and cross a river by its one shallow ford; elsewhere it's deep and slow. A base camp on the far bank has a tent, a lantern and a supply cache that never restocks. In the north is the final site, a ring of standing stones round a dark centre stone.
- The flow: at the stones, pressing 5 warns you that going further may be irreversible. Walk away and leave by the trailhead, and it's an ordinary extraction. Press 5 again to commit, and the way back is no longer the same. The base lantern is out, the tent has moved, and you hear things that aren't there: your own voice, a guide's whistle, footsteps keeping pace. Something very tall stands between the trees. The paper map stays truthful. At the last stretch before the trailhead it is waiting, and the screen cuts to black. That's the disappearance, recorded separately from death. Dying before that point is still an ordinary death you can retry.
- To see it: open the game with ?map=g09. The side panel shows the finale phase, the cache and what has changed. Keys: 5 at the stones (twice to commit), 6/7 take from the cache, H at the trailhead to leave.
- Checked in game, all passing: the ford wades and the river beside it is deep; the cache gives three food then stays empty; leaving after only the warning is an ordinary extraction; committing takes the warning plus a second deliberate press; on the return two landmarks had changed and false calls played while the map stayed the same; the encounter at the trailhead cut to black as a disappearance; and dying after committing (before the encounter) stayed an ordinary death.
