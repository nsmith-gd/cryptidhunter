- Health is now the only way to get hurt. A HEALTH bar sits above energy (out of 100, with "BLEEDING" and your first-aid kits), and a cryptid's contact is an injury instead of an instant end. Bigfoot or the Mothman leaves you at 40 health with a heavy bleeding wound; the test prowler 40 damage and the Chupacabra 30, each with a lighter wound. A red flash says e.g. "Bigfoot struck you! −60 health / Heavy bleeding: bandage it (B)".
- Each contact counts once: staying in reach doesn't stack hits for 2 seconds. Bleeding keeps draining health (heavy is about 1.5 a second) until you bandage it. Press B to use one of your three first-aid kits: after a moment the bleeding stops and you get 10 health back. The warning names the cause and the fix: "Heavy bleeding (Bigfoot): losing health. Bandage it (B)".
- The hunt ends only when health reaches 0, from a second big hit or a wound left to bleed out. Then you see the usual Caught screen, with that night's photos lost and your gear kept.
- How to see it: `npm run dev`, let Bigfoot reach you, then press B.
