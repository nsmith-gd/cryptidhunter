- New 4-slot loadout. The start screen shows a LOADOUT row under BASE CAMP listing all the gear you own (bait, trail cameras, audio lure, night-vision goggles, knock stick, spare batteries). Click an item to pack it or leave it at camp, up to 4. Your choice is remembered for the next hunt.
- In the woods the four slots sit bottom-right with counts ("1 Bait ×3", "4 Night vision ON"). Pick one with 1–4 or the mouse wheel, then use it with Q, or left-click while your camera is lowered. Each item's own key (G, T, L, V, K, R) still works if it's packed and jumps to its slot. Gear left at camp isn't carried at all; pressing its key just says "… is back at camp". Camera and flashlight are always with you and don't take a slot.
- Base camp now owns the full kit: 3 bait, 2 trail cameras, 1 audio lure, goggles, knock stick and 1 spare battery. It restocks the basics before each hunt. Only what you packed comes back changed.
- How to see it: `npm run dev`, click items in the LOADOUT row, start, then press 1–4 and Q.
- Verified (`npm run check:t27`, real clicks and keys): packing by clicking (a fifth item won't fit); each of the six items equipped and used across two hunts (bait dropped, trail camera set by left-click, lure calling, goggles on, knock answered, battery swapped); the wheel moves between slots; keys for unpacked gear do nothing. The wood-knock and night-vision checks now pack those items first. All other checks pass except the swamp check, which also times out on main and isn't caused by this. Captures in `.ballast/media/t27/`.
- Assumptions: no buying yet; the starter kit is free and topped up at camp. "Use" is Q because left-click belongs to the camera. Testers can add `?loadout=nightVision,knockStick,...` to the web address to pack specific gear.

## Questions
### Should the camera and flashlight take up loadout slots too?
Context: Right now you always have your camera and flashlight, and the four slots are only for extra gear (bait, trail cameras, lure, goggles, knock stick, spare batteries). That means you never have to choose between your light and, say, the goggles.
Options: - Camera and flashlight are free, 4 slots for gear (current: simple, never stranded without the basics)
- Flashlight takes a slot (goggles vs torch becomes a real choice)
- Both take slots, with more slots overall (fullest choice, more to manage)
Going with: camera and flashlight are free; the core verbs should always be available.
