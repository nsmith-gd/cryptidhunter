- New test gym, G06: observation network and adaptive threat. It's a camp in dense woods. Three cover corridors come at it from the north, and an alternate route loops round behind a big rock on the west (the blind spot). Three security cameras on posts each watch one corridor; a fourth sits where the rock blocks its view. A power hub feeds them, and a red alarm ring circles the camp. Each camera has a small light: green when it's working, dark when it's unpowered or removed.
- The Hidebehind stand-in (a tall pale shape) only moves through cover nobody is watching. Each step it checks whether you can see that stretch directly, or whether a working, powered camera with a clear view can. If the stretch is watched it waits; if the corridor is a dead end it backs out and tries another. It never jumps. When it crosses the ring, the alarm warns you before it reaches camp.
- Keys in the gym: 5/6/7 remove or restore camera A/B/C, 8 hub power, 9 recharge, 0 move camera D past the rock (the fix for the blind spot). The side panel shows each camera's state, the hub charge and the alarm. Adding &proxy=dogman swaps in the Dogman stand-in for the route-memory tests.
- Checked by the scripted run:
  - A dark or blocked camera covers nothing.
  - With full coverage it never advances onto a watched stretch and never reaches camp, and you sleep the night through without being woken.
  - Removing one camera opens a gap it uses. The alarm warned 17 s in, and restoring the camera stopped it short of camp.
  - When the power fails the cameras go dark, and the alarm still fired about a minute before it got in.
  - The Dogman learned nothing from a route walked twice behind the rock. It went to wait on the route it watched twice, so switching routes kept you far from it.
  - A reset replays identically.
- Assumptions: you see 32 m in a wide cone; cameras see 30 m in a narrow one, one per corridor. The alarm ring is 13 m. The "4 of 5 testers" criterion needs real players and is marked not evaluated. I also fixed a gap in route memory (#151) so a repeat walk sampled at a different moment still counts as the same route.
