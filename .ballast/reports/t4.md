## Questions
### Should raising the camera be held or toggled?
Context: Right now you hold the right mouse button to keep the camera up; letting go lowers it. While it's up the view zooms in, which narrows what you can see around you.
Options: - Hold to raise (current: quick to lower if something charges you)
- Click once to raise, click again to lower (easier on the hand for long stakeouts)
Going with: hold to raise; it matches aiming in most first-person games and keeps the tension of "camera up, guard down".

### Does a photo need to show most of the creature, or is any glimpse enough?
Context: Today a photo counts if any part of Bigfoot (even just its head over a wall) is visible in the frame. The photo already records how much of it was visible, ready for scoring later.
Options: - Any glimpse counts (current, forgiving)
- Need at least half of it visible (harder, rewards a clear line of sight)
- Any glimpse counts, but scores less (the planned scoring level)
Going with: any glimpse counts for now; scoring can reward a fuller shot later.
