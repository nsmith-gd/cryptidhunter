Added the missing GDD clue types to Bigfoot's trail in Pine Hollow: snapped branches (high, above head height) and droppings, alongside footprints, hair and scratch marks. Examining any of them with E gives a direction, and following that direction leads to fresher signs.
Deer tracks are scattered through the woods as false leads (4 trails per map). Up close they read "Small split-hoof prints: just a deer", give no direction, can't be collected, and don't change the journal's guess.
New evidence: X on droppings takes a sample in a vial (3 per hunt, about $90 at camp). Photographing a snapped branch counts as a disturbance photo.
How to see it: `npm run check:t84`. It fast-forwards Bigfoot 3 minutes and examines every clue type (all PASS). Captures are in `.ballast/media/t84/`: a snapped branch, droppings and deer tracks, each examined.
Assumptions: deer appear only in Pine Hollow for now. The other GDD evidence types (EV07 and up) belong to later levels and are not built.
