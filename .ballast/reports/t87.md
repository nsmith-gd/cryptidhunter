Added the GDD's early-level personal gear that wasn't in the game. Each item has a pack weight, a shop price from the GDD, and a use and effect in a Pine Hollow hunt:
- Binoculars (O): a narrow, magnified view through an eyepiece mask; raising the camera lowers them. They prove nothing by themselves.
- Signal whistle (Y): one blast heard about 60 m away. Bigfoot comes to check out the noise.
- Field recorder (P): while it runs, nearby whoops and wood knocks become sellable acoustic recordings. Quality is better closer and when you're quiet.
- Headlamp (I): wider, dimmer, shorter-reaching light that's easier on the battery.
- From a quick slot with Q: an emergency bivy (shelter and warmth while you stay put; moving packs it away), dry clothes (stand still a few seconds and your wetness is gone) and a fire kit (6 small fires that warm you and burn out after a few minutes).
The starter kit now includes dry clothes and a whistle; the rest is in the base-camp shop, and gear you keep can only be bought once. How to see it: `npm run check:t87` (11/11 PASS) buys the kit, uses every item with its real key and sells a recording; captures are in `.ballast/media/t87/`.
Assumptions: "every listed gear item" is scoped to personal gear for levels 01–03 that Pine Hollow's systems can use. Map, compass, first aid, food, the sample pouch, the casting kit, vials and trail cameras were already in.
Left out for now: thirst items (canteen, water purification) because there's no thirst system yet; tent, sleeping bag and cookware because there's no sleep or cooking; and later-level gear (boats, power, capture, security). The headlamp still shows the hand torch in your hand.

## Questions
### How far should the signal whistle carry, and should it scare a cryptid away instead of drawing it in?
Context: one blast of the whistle is heard about 60 m around you. A Bigfoot that hasn't noticed you walks over to investigate. One that's very close usually spots you and bolts. It's useful to lure it or to tell it where you are, and dangerous if you're hiding.
Options: - As now: it draws curious cryptids in from afar
- Make it a scare tool: a blast up close always sends it running
- Shorter range (about 30 m), so it's a local signal only
Going with: as now, because the GDD calls it "can signal or distract; noisy".
