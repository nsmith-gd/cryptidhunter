## Questions
### Should trails be slightly sunken ruts, or stay flat and painted?
Context: Trails are now worn dirt painted into the forest floor, with grassy shoulders and ragged edges, all on one flat surface. Real game trails are often a few centimetres lower than the ground beside them, which catches shadows and makes them easier to follow at night.
Options: - Flat and painted (current: clean, no risk of footprints or feet floating) - Shallow ruts, a few cm deep (more depth and shadow; the ground under clues would need re-fitting) - Ruts plus roots and stones crossing the path (most detailed, more work)
Going with: Flat and painted for now; it fixes the flicker without touching how clues and characters stand on the ground.
