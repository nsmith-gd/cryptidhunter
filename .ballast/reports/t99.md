STATUS: DONE
- Every photo and piece of evidence you bring home is now graded at HQ from 0 (Inconclusive) to 4 (Exceptional), using how clear, identifiable, complete and well-documented it is. Each entry comes with a plain reason, such as "too dark", "blurry", "subject out of frame" or "clear and complete".
- HQ also checks what really made each piece of evidence. Pine Hollow now has ordinary wildlife false leads: deer hoof prints, red fox paw prints and owl feathers. In the field they look like any other sign. At HQ they are rejected, however sharp the photo ("ordinary animal: a red fox, not Bigfoot"), and pay $0.
- Payouts now follow the GDD offer: level maximum × 1% × the type's base units × grade multiplier × novelty. At Pine Hollow an exceptional Bigfoot photo pays $88 and a hair sample pays $30. Repeat sightings of the same type pay half, then a quarter. Several frames of one sighting count as one event and are paid once.
- The results screen has an HQ VERIFICATION list: every entry with its grade (or REJECTED), the reason and what it paid. The field and the extraction manifest show no dollar amounts.
- Checked in game: a clear, close photo of a fox track is rejected at HQ with its reason; a photo taken with the torch off grades Weak/Inconclusive with "too dark"; a lit, sharp photo pays the GDD offer. Unit tests pass.
- Assumptions: physical samples have no measured quality yet, so they're graded Compelling (the GDD's own $30 example). Thresholds are read literally: 19 is grade 0 and 20 is grade 1. Bounties and reputation still use the photo score.
