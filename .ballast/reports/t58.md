## Questions
### Should Bigfoot's face be bigger and more human, like the reference, or stay a small, hooded, ape-like face?
Context: Up close in torchlight you now see a tan, scowling face sunk under a heavy brow and a hood of hair, with glowing amber eyes. It reads as Bigfoot from 6 m away. The reference has a larger, flatter, more human face with readable features.
Options: - Bigger, more human face: more readable and more unsettling up close, but less animal at a distance
- Keep the small, hooded, ape-like face: more of a silhouette and glowing eyes, with less face detail
Going with: keep it as it is for now, because most sightings happen at 10 m or more in the dark, where the eyes and silhouette carry the scare.
