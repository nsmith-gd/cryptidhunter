## Questions
### Should Bigfoot's limbs move in crisp stepped poses or smoothly?
Context: His body now snaps between key poses 12 times a second, like hand-animated pixel art, while he glides smoothly across the ground. It reads chunky and stylised up close; at a distance you mostly notice it as a slightly jerky, heavy gait.
Options: - Stepped at 12 poses a second (current: pixel-art feel, a little uncanny) - Smooth (natural, more like a regular 3D game) - Stepped slower, 8 a second (very stylised, more creepy and puppet-like)
Going with: 12 a second, which matches the pixel style and gives his movement an unsettling, deliberate weight.
