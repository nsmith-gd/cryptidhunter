STATUS: DONE
- Added a Chupacabra stand-in: a low brown box (about knee height) with yellow eyes that starts out behind you and to the right in the test map.
- It only moves while you're not looking at it. Turn away, or lose sight of it behind a box, and it creeps toward you a bit slower than walking pace. Look at it and it freezes.
- Once it's within 6 m and unseen, it crouches flat for a split second and then charges at about three times sprint speed. Catching it during that crouch stops the charge; once it leaps, it's committed.
- A hit flashes the screen red with "CHARGED!" and shoves you back. It then runs off about 16 m and starts stalking again. There's no health or death yet.
- To see it: `npm run dev`, click to play, turn around to find the eyes, then look away and wait. Screenshots in `.ballast/media/t8/` (watching it at 5 m, mid-charge, the hit).
- Assumptions: it's in the shared greybox test map, since no desert/ranch area exists yet; "watching" means it's near the middle of your view and not hidden behind something (your flashlight doesn't matter); a hit has no lasting cost yet.
- PR: https://github.com/nsmith-gd/cryptidhunter/pull/5

## Questions
### Should simply looking at the Chupacabra freeze it, or does it need to be in your flashlight beam?
Context: Right now, if it's roughly in the middle of your view and nothing is in the way, it freezes, even in near darkness. In the dark map it's hard to make out unless your light is on it, so it can feel like you're freezing something you can barely see.
Options: - Looking is enough (current: fair and readable, a little forgiving) - Only the flashlight beam freezes it (scarier, and pairs with Mothman being drawn to the light) - Looking slows it, the beam stops it (a middle ground)
Going with: Looking is enough, the simplest rule to test at greybox.
