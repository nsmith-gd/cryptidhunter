The Sidehill Gouger (Level 2, Oro-Medonte) is now its GDD creature, built from data on the shared cryptid AI. It grazes along one height round the hills (a contour), always one way, and its acute hearing picks you up. Once you're well into its woods and not looking at it, it shadows you from the slope. It starts about 40 m off and closes to about 15 m as dusk falls and the further you are from the car.
Turn and look at it and it freezes: "Something low on the slope froze the moment you looked. It's been following you." Stare too long or walk at it and it bolts along the slope, the way it can run and you can't. It never attacks.
Evidence: its tracks come in lopsided pairs (one side 9 cm, the other 13 cm: the same side is always uphill). The journal describes them that way and now counts the Gouger as a suspect. The trail reader gives a direction, and it keeps going that way, so you can predict its route. A photo of it walking across a slope is a "Hillside gait photo", its distinctive-locomotion evidence ($35 base). Tufts of hair as before.
Fixed for hills along the way: the camera aimed at a cryptid's height above zero rather than above its own ground, and cryptid eyes and line of sight ignored the ground they stand on.
How to see it: `?map=oromedonte`, then go north past the meadow, look away for a while, and turn round (F4 shows the STALK state). `npm run check:t130` (6/6 PASS) covers grazing on its contour, route prediction from its prints, stalking that closes in as dusk falls, freezing when watched and then bolting, and the gait photo taken with the real camera buttons. Captures are in `.ballast/media/t130/`.
Assumptions: it only stalks once you're about a third of the way from the car toward the far edge of its woods. "Watched" means near the middle of your view with a clear line of sight. Stalking and contour walking are shared AI actions, so other creatures can use them from data.
Regression: t86, t21, t129 and the smoke test pass. t22 (the AI debug panel and "gives up the chase") and t84 (the snapped-branch photo) fail on main too.

## Questions
### How close should the Gouger get when it stalks you at dusk?
Context: once you're deep in the woods, it follows you along the slope while you aren't looking: about 40 m back in the afternoon, closing to about 15 m by dusk. Turn round and you catch a low shape that freezes, then bolts if you stare or walk at it.
Options: - As now, 40 m closing to 15 m: you catch it once or twice a trip
- Closer, 25 m closing to 8 m: creepier, and you see it more often
- Further, 60 m closing to 25 m: rarer and harder to spot
Going with: 40 m to 15 m, so it's seen at least once a trip without becoming a constant presence.
