New test gym G05 "Water, weather and power": open `?map=g05` (add `&storm` for the storm variant, `&blind` to hide the answers). It has a lake with deep water in the middle, a boat at a mooring by the dock, a sonar buoy out on the water, a charging station with a generator, a heated hut on the west shore and a dry exit you can walk to.
Controls: E boards or leaves the boat, W/S/A/D drive it, 5 engine, 6 sonar ping, 7 copy the buoy's data, 8 generator. "Run script" plays the whole test and shows each pass criterion on screen.
What it proves, in calm and storm: fuel drops once for the engine's burn; each sonar ping costs battery once; a flat battery turns off only the sonar and GPS (torch, paper map and engine keep working); the generator recharges it; an empty tank stalls the engine and you paddle slowly; going over the side in deep water runs down your breath, soaks and chills you, and hurts you only through health (drowning). The hut warms you back up. Only data you copied comes home. A creature under the water homes in on the engine and loses interest when you cut it.
Storm (`&storm`): rain, darker, colder air, the engine burns 1.5× the fuel and the wind pushes the boat. Reset reloads and replays to the identical state; blind mode hides the creature and the trace.
Assumptions: the deep-water proxy stands in for the L05 Mugwump. "Testers can say what failed" is left not_evaluated because it needs real playtesters. Fault messages always say the cause and the way to recover.
Captures and the full results (gym-results.json) are in `.ballast/media/t138/`. Run `npm run check:t138` to repeat it.
